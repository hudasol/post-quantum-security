Author: Huda Mueen. Generated by `scripts/summarize_verification6.py`; do not edit by hand.

Round 5 staged 265 evidence rows from 16 sources (`data/raw/S13_staged.csv`). Each row was read against its source by two readers who saw neither the staged values nor each other's sheets (blank sheets from `scripts/verify6_sheets.py`, rules in `data/verify6/READING_RULES.md`). Every value disagreement and every wording disagreement was sent to a third reader who saw only the row id, the field, the source and the location (`data/verify6/third_look_queue.csv`); verdicts are in `data/verify6/third_look.csv`. The comparison rules are those of round 4 (`scripts/summarize_verification5.py`) with two changes. Sending every disagreement to the third look was fixed before the comparison ran. A list of wording synonyms (for example keygen for 'Key Generation' or 'Keypair'), used when a third reading is compared with staging, was added after the first comparison had produced the list of wording disagreements and before the third look answers were read; the wording rate is given below with and without it.
