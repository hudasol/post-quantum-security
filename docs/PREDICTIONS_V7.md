# Predictions for round 7

Author: Huda Mueen. Written 2026-11-02, before any build or run of round 7, and hashed in `docs/PREDICTIONS_V7.md.sha256`. These predictions were registered after the original fourteen of round 6 (`docs/PREDICTIONS_V6.md`, of which twelve concern results in the paper) and after design K, which was itself added after the round 6 registration. They are scored by script after the runs (`scripts/score_predictions_v7.py`); misses stay. Verdicts: hit when the prediction holds; miss when the refutation clause is met; between when neither holds; not scored when the metric could not be computed as registered, with the reason. When a clause is ambiguous the less favourable reading is used and named.

What I already knew when writing: everything committed up to the commit that adds this file, including the round 6 RTL cycles (ML-DSA-44 per command: ground signing about 9.6 million cycles and vehicle verification about 5.4 million on Ibex), the round 6 gateway emulation results on 20 paired missions (ML-DSA-44 per command: round trip median about 510 ms, share acknowledged within 0.5 s about 0.42, about 25 more missed commands per mission than the hybrid session with the published rule; strict rule with a new handshake +1.60 and strict rule in session +0.95 missed commands per mission against the published rule; replay cache 0 against the published rule), and the published Falcon-512 signature size of 666 B (`data/raw/S15_signature_sizes.csv`). I had not built or run Falcon-512 on any core, had not run any burst loss model and had not run more than 20 missions per configuration.

Definitions. "Falcon-512" is the padded Falcon-512 implementation (fixed 666 B signatures) of the pinned library recorded in `docs/LICENSES.md`, built with integer emulated floating point, since the RV32IMC cores have no floating point unit. "Per command" means the design of round 6 in which the ground signs each command and the vehicle verifies it, with or without a 6 B counter. Cycles are medians over the 15 runs of an image. "Missed commands per mission" is the round 6 metric: stream commands not acknowledged within 0.5 s, outages included, per mission. A "difference" of missed commands is paired by mission seed, with a 95 percent bootstrap interval computed as in `sitl/analyse_r6.py`. "Burst loss" is the two state (Gilbert Elliott) loss model of round 7 with a mean frame loss of 0.02; "independent loss" is the round 6 model with a frame loss of 0.02. "Larger set" is the round 7 run of 100 missions per configuration under independent loss.
