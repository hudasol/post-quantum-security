# Gateway emulation with ArduPilot SITL, round 7

Author: Huda Mueen. Generated by `sitl/make_doc_r7.py` from `sitl/results_r7/`; do not edit by hand. The round 6 study (`docs/SITL_STUDY_R6.md`, `sitl/results_r6/`, `sitl/raw_r6/`) is unchanged; round 7 adds three result sets beside it, each in `sitl/raw_r7/<set>/`.

Set up as in round 6: ArduPilot Copter 4.7.2 in software in the loop with its built in vehicle model flies a scripted mission; the ground station sends one acknowledged COMMAND_LONG per second; a gateway pair executes the cryptography with pinned libraries in front of the unmodified flight stack (which never runs it); a relay emulates a SiK class radio with link layer retransmission and one outage per mission; vehicle side compute is a delay from Ibex cycles at the mission study clock. Labels: shares, round trips and missed commands are SITL measured; channel counts and handshake times emulated; delays estimated from RTL simulated cycles; host compute host measured. Intervals: 95 percent bootstrap over missions, paired by seed for differences (`sitl/analyse_r7.py`). Missed commands per mission: stream commands not acknowledged within 0.5 s, outages included (the round 6 metric).

## Result sets

- falcon20: Falcon-512 per command, 20 paired missions, independent loss. Compared with the round 6 runs on the same seeds.
- burst20: two state (burst) loss, 20 paired missions. The two state loss process (`sitl/linkrelay.py`) moves between a good and a bad state once per transmitted frame and loses every frame sent in the bad state: p(good to bad) 0.00408, p(bad to good) 0.20, so the mean frame loss is 0.02 (the round 6 value) and the mean burst 5 frames (`sitl/settings_r7.csv`, an assumption without a measured SiK source; one value, no sweep). Outages are as in round 6. Compared with the round 6 runs (and falcon20 for Falcon-512) under independent loss on the same seeds.
- large100: larger set, 100 paired missions, independent loss. Missions 0 to 19 repeat the round 6 seeds.
