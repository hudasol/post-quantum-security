# Verification 5: two independent readers for every round 4 row

Author: Huda Mueen. Generated by `scripts/summarize_verification5.py`; do not edit by hand.

Round 4 staged 483 evidence rows and 63 link and timing facts. Each was read against its source by two readers who saw neither the staged values nor each other's sheets (blank sheets from `scripts/verify5_sheets.py`). Disagreements on values all got a third look; a seeded sample of wording disagreements got one too (`data/verify5/adjudication.csv`). The comparison rules of this round 4 comparison are in the script header and were fixed before that comparison ran (the round 5 third look below used rules written later).

Limits. Only 60 of 453 wording disagreements (operation, scheme, library, conditions and similar fields) got a third look, and staging was wrong in 6 of them. If that share holds for the rest, roughly 39 wording fields of the promoted rows are still wrong; values are not affected, but groupings that use these fields (the meta analysis) may be. Two readers also wrote helper scripts to the same scratch path by accident (`docs/RUN_LOG.md` entry 35); each sheet came from its own fetches, but shared tooling cannot be excluded.

Round 5 third look. Every remaining wording disagreement got a third reading by a reader who saw only the source, the row id, the field and the location (`stats/third_look_classify.py`, `docs/STATISTICS_V5.md` section 4). The comparison of those readings was not blind and not fixed in advance: the match rules R1 to R5 were written after a first literal comparison had shown that almost every line differed in wording, and the substance decisions for conditions, library, quantity and unit (`data/verify5/third_look_manual.csv`) were made with all values in view. Staged wording differs verbatim from the source in 153 of 365 settled lines. It contradicts the source in substance in 1 of 365 settled lines of this round (0.3 percent); the automatic test behind this figure is lenient (a scheme is compared only by family, a parameter set only when no number is shared, and 41 of 65 differing lines of the judged fields could not be mapped and count as no contradiction). The round 4 sample, judged under the stricter round 4 rule, gave 6 of 60 (10.0 percent). Pooled, the two give 7 of 425 (1.6 percent, Wilson 95 percent interval 0.8 to 3.4 percent), but the two tests differ, so the pooled figure mixes two definitions. This replaces the extrapolation in the limits paragraph above.

Only confirmed and corrected rows enter `data/raw/S12_verified.csv` (`scripts/promote_s12.py`); single reader and blocked rows stay in staging with their reason in `data/verify5/row_status.csv`.
