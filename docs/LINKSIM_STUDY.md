## 4. Validation against a published link figure

Target: Table 5 of ePrint 2026/2343, TLS 1.3 handshake over an emulated UAV profile (57.6 kbps, 40 ms delay), rows S10F-131 to S10F-150 of `data/evidence.csv`. Model: published clear link time plus 2 round trips of 2 x 40 ms plus bytes over rate; variant `tcp` adds 52 B per 1448 B segment (both ASSUMPTIONS). Error = predicted / published minus 1. This table deliberately sets published values (labelled published, with row ids) beside estimated ones (labelled predicted) because its purpose is to measure the model's error. Mean absolute error of the absolute times: 33.3% (plain), 31.0% (with TCP headers), over 5 groups.

Limits: the paper does not say whether netem delayed one or both directions, the TLS stack and TCP behaviour (slow start, acknowledgements) are not modelled, and the clear link time stands for all fixed costs. The check validates the bytes over rate part of the model on one bandwidth bound link; it does not validate the duty cycle, fragmentation or loss parts, for which no published figure is in the evidence table. In short, the rate part is consistent with one published link, not validated in general.

| SMAUG-T3 | test | 923 | 916 | -0.7% |

This is a weak test: all five groups differ only in key exchange bytes (1,338 to 3,504 B on the wire) on one emulated link, so a single constant can absorb TCP and TLS behaviour that would not carry over to another link or object size.

### 4.2 Cross check against a prose figure (not calibration)

ePrint 2021/781, pages 15 to 16, prose (not a table; not in the evidence table) states that 50 kB take 30 to 60 s over a low power IEEE 802.15.4 link, depending on link quality and network load. The model, with no loss and selective repeat on the 802.15.4 profile, gives 4.5 s for 50000 B, a factor of 6.6 below the low end. The model leaves out multi hop forwarding, medium access contention, 6LoWPAN headers and network load, so on 802.15.4 networks its times are optimistic by that order. The figure is not in `data/evidence.csv`, because it is not in a table.
