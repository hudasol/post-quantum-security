# Licence decision

Author: Huda Mueen. Decided 2026-10-12.

The author chose no open licence. Anyone who wants to use, copy, modify, distribute or sell any part of this repository must contact her first. She expects to agree to reasonable requests and would like to know where the work is useful. The wording is in `LICENSE`; `NOTICE` gives the short form.

Points to know:

1. This is not open source. Without a licence, copyright law gives others no right to reuse the work, and that is the intent. GitHub's Terms of Service still let people view and fork a public repository on GitHub.
2. The numbers in `data/evidence.csv` are facts copied from third party sources, each with its URL. The notice covers the author's selection, structure, notes and labels, not the underlying papers. No paper is stored in this repository.
3. Third party libraries are never committed; they are downloaded by script and keep their own licences (`docs/LICENSES.md`). Two of them (wolfSSL, wolfBoot) are GPL-3.0.
4. The notice is plain wording, not legal advice. If a university, employer or funder has a claim on the work, check that before relying on it.
5. Contact is through the GitHub profile https://github.com/hudasol. No email address is published here.
