# Verification, third pass (rows from author supplied PDFs)

Author: Huda Mueen. Generated by `scripts/summarize_verification3.py`; do not edit by hand.

## Method

Each of the six staged files `data/raw/S10?_*.csv` was rechecked in full by a fresh reader that had not seen the extraction notes. The reader rendered each table page of the author supplied PDF, read every cell, recomputed every stated unit conversion and judged four things per row: the value, the location (page, table, row, column), the row description (scheme, parameter set, operation, device) and whether any statement in the warning is false. Results: `data/verify3/<set>_results.csv`. `scripts/promote_s10.py` applies only the fixes those results call for and writes `data/raw/S10_verified.csv`, which `scripts/merge_evidence.py` merges as stream S10.

Row error rate: 41 of 403 (10.2 percent; Wilson 95 percent interval 7.6 to 13.5 percent).

Kinds of error found:

1. Mapping, 38 rows (S10D): the paper reports signing and verification cost in KiloTicks and says ticks come from the hardware clock and the time spent. The extraction multiplied them by 1000 and put them in the cycles column. Every printed number was right, but the paper does not establish that a tick is one CPU cycle, so the reader marked the value as not supported. Fix: the cycles cell is empty and the KiloTicks stay verbatim in the warning.
2. Warning text, 3 rows (S10E-002, S10E-006, S10E-008): the warning described what a Table 9 figure covers wrongly (sampling by the hash accelerator was included; one gadget runs over k plus 1 polynomials). Fix: the sentence is replaced.

Transcription errors (a printed number copied wrongly): 0 of 403. With zero errors the rule of three gives a 95 percent upper bound of 3/403 = 0.74 percent; the Wilson upper bound is 0.94 percent.

## Limits

1. One fresh reader per set; a second independent reader was not used, so errors that two readers would both miss are not bounded by this check.
2. The readers followed the same reading method as the extractor (page renders, cell by cell) and were briefed separately; they are independent of the extraction notes, not of every habit of that method.
3. The S10 rows sit outside the phase 1 and phase 2 verification rates in `docs/VERIFICATION.md` and `docs/VERIFICATION2.md`.
4. Prior works quoted inside RISQrypt (ePrint 2026/312) were checked against RISQrypt's tables, not against the original papers.
