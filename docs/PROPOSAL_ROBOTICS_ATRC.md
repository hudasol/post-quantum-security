# Proposal summary for TII and ATRC: post quantum ready command and update channels for drones and robots

Author: Huda Mueen. Two page summary of `docs/PROPOSAL_ROBOTICS.md`, generated by `scripts/build_paper.py`; every number is read from a result file.

This is a student research proposal and a seed for discussion. It is not a deployment, it has no relationship with or endorsement by any organisation named, and organisation facts come only from the public pages listed in `docs/CONTEXT_ENTITIES.md`.

## Problem

Drones and robots authenticate commands and firmware on microcontroller flight computers over narrow links. The post quantum replacements are standardised, but their cost on such devices and links, and which devices must be replaced rather than updated, are not yet measured.

## Why this may be relevant (public facts only)

- The TII Cryptography Research Center page lists classical and post quantum cryptography research, lightweight cryptography, cryptographic implementation on custom hardware, and quantum key distribution and random number generation among its activities. (TII-02)
- The TII Secure Systems Research Center page states a focus on end to end security and resilience for cyber physical and autonomous systems, ranging from secure phones to autonomous drones, with research cards on secure mesh networking and a zero trust approach to autonomous systems. (TII-04)
- A TII news item dated April 2021 lists Secure Systems Research Center projects with universities covering drone communication security, secure mesh communication under power limits, physical layer security for UAV to ground links, and secure onboard computation for resource constrained nano drones. (TII-05)
- An ATRC news item dated May 2026 reports an agreement between the UAE Cyber Security Council and ATRC entities for a national post quantum migration plan, using TII national cryptographic libraries (classical and post quantum) and quantum key distribution, and stated to be aligned with NIST post quantum standards; embedded and IoT devices are not mentioned. (TII-06)
- The TII Partnerships and Programmes page lists a visiting scholars programme, a graduate programme for postgraduate students, year long internships open to undergraduate and graduate students, planned dual degree programmes, and research partnerships with universities; it gives no open call or deadline. (TII-07)

## What already exists

- 1,328 published rows with provenance, every new row read by two independent readers.
- A cycle model with a median held out error of 1.0 percent within one code base (modelled), and board predictions registered in advance.
- A mission simulation: with a hybrid session, command availability on a telemetry radio stays at 0.992; a signature on every command lowers it to 0.794 and adds about 380 ms per command (estimated).
- Firmware verification of a 1 MB image with LMS: about 470 ms with a software hash, 30 ms with hash hardware (estimated).
- Known gap: the link model is 4.2 times faster than a published 802.15.4 transfer time.

## Proposed next step

A short board session and a hardware in the loop study with a borrowed flight controller and telemetry radio, scored against predictions registered in this repository, followed by a reference design note for a session based command and update channel (`docs/REFERENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md`).
