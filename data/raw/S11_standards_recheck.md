## 2. Narrative by jurisdiction

### Canada
The April 2026 date has passed. Neither primary source reports whether departments met it, so the report can say only what was required. The Cyber Centre roadmap is unchanged since its first release. The Treasury Board notice of 9 October 2025 is the binding text and adds two later steps that the S1 pass did not record: a Phase 2 plan update by 1 April 2027 and, by 1 April 2028, a Phase 3 plan update with the start of system transitions. It also requires PQC procurement clauses in digital contracts signed after 1 April 2026, with CMVP certified modules and crypto agility. Nothing in either source marks these dates as provisional, so the *in flux* flag no longer applies to the Canadian dates themselves; what is unknown is compliance.

### Singapore
The consultation drafts have been replaced by Version 1 of both the handbook and the readiness index (16 July 2026). Version 1 adds three dated milestones for CII owners (plan to CSA by 31 March 2027, new systems quantum safe or ready from 1 January 2028, migration complete by 31 December 2031) and an algorithm table that limits LMS and XMSS to firmware signing. The handbook calls itself non mandatory, while calling the milestones requirements, and says detailed guidance for the milestones is still being written. The item therefore stays *in flux*, for a new reason.

### Germany (BSI)
Unchanged since 2026-10-11: TR-02102 is at version 2026-01, and the two NIST curve hybrid groups from RFC 10024 are promised for the next TR-02102-2. The one point the S1 row did not record is that BSI already treats them as usable in a TR compliant way in TLS 1.3. The next version has no date, so the item stays *in flux*.

### Australia (ASD)
Not rechecked. cyber.gov.au could not be reached with WebFetch on 2026-10-17. Search results suggest the page has moved and that a newer ISM edition exists, so the S1 summary may be stale. The item stays *in flux* and marked as read only through a summary.
