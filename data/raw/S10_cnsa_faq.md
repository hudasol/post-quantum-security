# S10 NSA CNSA 2.0 and Quantum Computing FAQ (raw notes)

Stream: S10. Author of review: Huda Mueen. Source: NSA Cybersecurity Information Sheet "The Commercial National Security Algorithm Suite 2.0 and Quantum Computing FAQ", supplied by the author as a PDF (21 pages). Read: 2026-10-16 (Asia/Dubai). Date and version as printed on every page footer: December 2024, Ver. 2.1 (document identifiers U/OO/194427-22 and PP-24-4014). The separate NSA CNSA 2.0 algorithms advisory was NOT supplied and is not used in these notes; nothing below is drawn from it.

Rules applied: PROTOCOL.md sections 4 and 7 (only pages actually read; "in flux" flagged; at most one short quote). Page numbers are the printed page numbers, which match the PDF page index (checked on rendered pages 1, 3, 11 and 12).

Legend: Status "Final" means the statement is given in the published Ver. 2.1 FAQ without a caveat. "IN FLUX" means the FAQ itself says the point is an expectation, applies only for the present, depends on future guidance, profiles or standards, or could change. NSS = National Security Systems.

## 2. Gaps and limits of this source

- The FAQ gives only general milestones (2025-12-31, 2027-01-01, 2030-12-31, 2031-12-31, 2035) plus the 2025 expectation for firmware roots of trust (row 20). It does not give separate dates for software and firmware signing, networking equipment, operating systems, browsers and servers, or niche, custom and legacy equipment. Per category milestones were not found in this document and cannot be cited from S10; they would need the separate advisory or CNSSP 15, neither of which was supplied.
- The FAQ does not mention microcontrollers, drones or robots. Rows 14 to 21, 29 and 35 are the parts closest to embedded systems.
- The section list on page 1 names a Preparation section, but no section with that heading appears in the body text read.

## 3. Use in this review

This note can support statements about which post quantum algorithms and parameter levels NSA approves for US national security systems, the published CNSA 2.0 milestone dates, the preference for LMS or XMSS in firmware roots of trust with signing and state kept in hardware, and NSA's position that hybrids are not required and generally discouraged. It cannot support claims that these algorithms, parameters or dates bind other states, civil drones and robots, or commercial systems outside NSS, since the FAQ itself limits its requirements to NSS. Per category transition dates and anything from the separate CNSA 2.0 advisory must be sourced elsewhere.
